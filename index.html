Josie

I am a first-year master's student at the Computer Vision Group, The University of Tokyo, advised by Prof. Yoichi Sato. I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Ritsumeikan University (Osaka) in March 2026, where I was also a visiting student at CUHK-Shenzhen working on multi-agent prompt optimization.

I have always been curious about a simple question: Why do humans understand so well, yet machines — despite their scale — still don't? My research explores how ML systems can develop more structured, interpretable, and multimodal forms of understanding, and how to design model and agent architectures that better reflect how humans organize knowledge.

My interests span multimodal video understanding, structured representations, LLM agents, and prompt optimization. Outside research, I write piano pieces, take photographs, and travel. Creative archive on Bilibili.
